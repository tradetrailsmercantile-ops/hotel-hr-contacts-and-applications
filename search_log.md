## Important interpretation

Job-board search totals are **discovery counts, not verified suitable jobs**. Counts vary as sites refresh and can include duplicate ads, stale/expired listings, jobs requiring Finnish or experience, roles requiring an existing Finnish work/residence permit, and part-time contracts that cannot support the project's standard TTOL income/hour checks. Each promising result must still be checked against the verification policy.

## Search coverage and limits

This is a materially wider search than the first 12 records, but it is **still not an exhaustive export of every Finnish vacancy**. Search-result totals are not unique-job totals and do not indicate how many employers will hire a worker currently in India. Many listings have Finnish-language requirements, require existing experience or a residence/work permit, disclose no usable wage/hours, or cannot be verified beyond an aggregator excerpt.

The next pass should prioritise direct vacancy pages and official employer careers pages for leads that show an advertised base wage and enough regular hours, then ask the employer the same decisive question in writing: **Will you consider hiring a non-EU/EEA applicant who is currently outside Finland, and will you provide the employer-side information needed for the correct work-based residence-permit application?**

No applications have been submitted and no employer has confirmed overseas hiring in the records unless the CSV explicitly states otherwise.
