## Important interpretation

Job-board search totals are **discovery counts, not verified suitable jobs**. They can include duplicate ads, stale/expired listings, jobs requiring Finnish or experience, roles requiring an existing Finnish work/residence permit, and part-time contracts that cannot support the project's standard TTOL income/hour checks. Each promising result must still be checked against the verification policy.
